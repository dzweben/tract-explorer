Node-wise Tract Explorer

A reusable, in-browser viewer for along-tract (node-wise) statistical results. Drop in a results file and browse it: filter by any dimension in your data, click a row for the node profile, clusters, laterality, and stats. Nothing is uploaded — files are parsed locally in your browser.

Data format
Fit your data to the sample CSV. One row per node. Six required columns — outcome, tract, metric, node, t, p — everything else optional. Your permutation output already has the node-wise t and p; you just stack your analyses into one file and label each row with its outcome, tract, and metric. Column names are case-insensitive (Node, t_value, p_value etc. are fine as-is).

Results

FWE = cluster passes the permutation extent threshold (from your extent_threshold / passed columns). Direction: Positive higher metric → higher outcome · Negative higher metric → lower outcome. Node values are whatever statistic you supplied in t (partial-regression t recommended). Where hemispheres exist, the detail view stacks L and R aligned by node; nodes significant on both sides are green.
